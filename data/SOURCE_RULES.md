# Educational and AI source rules

1. Confirm textbook edition, official syllabus and chapter mapping for the target cohort.
2. Record source title, edition, page and section for factual lesson content.
3. Verify historical board questions against original papers before publishing.
4. Never invent past-paper provenance, question numbers, exam frequency or predictions.
5. Clearly label AI-generated practice separately from verified historical questions.
6. AI-generated explanations may be wrong; review formulas, worked examples and translations before teaching.
7. Do not submit student personal/sensitive information to an external AI service.
8. The AI tutor may use selected saved lesson content as context; it is not an independent source verification system.
9. Respect copyright and permissions for textbooks and past papers.
